Getting Here

🚗 By Car
🚇 By Transit
✈️ From Airport
Take the Grand District exit from the City Ring Road and follow signs for Velora Boulevard. Complimentary valet parking is available at our main entrance. Secure self-parking is also available in our underground garage at $30/day for hotel guests.
Take the Metro Blue Line to Grand Central Station (Exit B). Hotel Velora is a 5-minute walk along Velora Boulevard. Our complimentary shuttle runs every 20 minutes from the station between 7AM and 11PM daily.
Hotel Velora is 28 minutes from International Airport via the express highway. Our luxury airport transfer service is available from $80 each way. Contact our concierge at least 24 hours in advance to arrange your private transfer.
