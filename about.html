Our Story

100 Years of Excellence
A Century of Luxury

Where Every Guest
Becomes a Legend

Hotel Velora was born in 1924 from a singular vision: to create not merely a place to sleep, but a world apart — a refuge of extraordinary beauty and impeccable grace in the heart of the city.

"We are not in the business of accommodation. We are in the business of memory."
— Edmond Velora, Founder

Founded by Swiss hotelier Edmond Velora, the hotel was constructed over three years using the finest Italian marble, hand-crafted fixtures, and materials sourced from across five continents. Its original Art Deco façade remains one of the city's most celebrated architectural landmarks.

Through nearly a century of history — weathering wars, economic shifts, and generational change — Hotel Velora has never wavered from its founding promise: that every guest, without exception, deserves to feel like the most important person in the world.

Defining Moments

Grand Opening

Edmond Velora opens the hotel on 14th April, 1924. The inaugural dinner is attended by royalty, diplomats, and the city's most distinguished citizens.

1924
1938

The Aurum Opens

Chef Pierre Fontaine opens the Aurum Restaurant to immediate acclaim, earning its first Michelin star within eighteen months of opening.

The Velora Wing

A new wing adds 40 rooms and the legendary Penthouse suite, designed by celebrated architect Renata Morelli in a seamless blend of heritage and modernity.

1962
1985

Forbes Five Stars

Hotel Velora receives its Forbes Travel Guide Five-Star rating for the first time — a distinction it has held every year since without interruption.

Spa & Rooftop Pool

A landmark renovation introduces the Velora Spa and rooftop infinity pool on the 22nd floor, redefining urban luxury hospitality worldwide.

2007
2024

Centenary & Renewal

Hotel Velora celebrates its centennial year with a comprehensive renovation, preserving all original heritage features while introducing contemporary luxury throughout.
